# DishAdvisor Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026.

## Who we are

DishAdvisor ("the app", "we", "us") is operated by **BAJAJTECHSOLUTIONZ LLC**, a Michigan limited
liability company. DishAdvisor helps you find well-reviewed dishes, not just well-reviewed
restaurants. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how it's used. It is part
of our [Terms of Service](/terms).

## Information we collect

**Account information.** How you sign in determines what we receive:

- **Email and password:** your email address. Your password is handled by our authentication
  provider, Supabase Auth, and stored only in hashed form — we never see it.
- **Sign in with Google:** your Google account's email address, name, and profile picture.
- **Sign in with Apple:** the email Apple shares with us (which may be a private-relay address)
  and, the first time you sign in, the name you choose to share.

Your name becomes your public display name and, if Google provides one, your profile picture is
shown with your reviews.

**Food preferences.** The spice tolerance, allergens to avoid, and favorite cuisines you choose.
These are private: they are never shown on your public profile or to other users, and are used
only to filter and rank dishes for you.

**Location.** With your permission, the app uses your device's location while you are using it
to find dishes near you. Your coordinates are sent with each search and, when we look for new
restaurants nearby, to Google Places. If you search a different area by name, your device's
built-in geocoding service (Apple or Google) converts that name to coordinates. We do not store
your location or keep a location history. You can turn location off in your device settings;
you can still search by restaurant name, but nearby results won't be available.

**Content you create.** Star ratings, written reviews, and photos you attach to a review are
public and shown with your display name. Dishes you save as favorites are private to you.

**Reports and blocks.** When you report a review, we record the review, the reason you chose, and
your account. When you block someone, we record that you blocked them. See "Reporting content"
below.

**Search choices.** Your searches and the filters you pick (such as price or party size) are
sent to our servers to return results. They are not saved to your account.

**Usage data.** We do not use any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK. Our hosting
providers keep standard server logs (such as request times and IP addresses) for security and
troubleshooting. If we ever add an analytics or crash-reporting service, we will update this policy before it
ships.

**Restaurant and dish data.** Restaurant details (name, address, hours, price level) come from
Google Places and Yelp. Dish descriptions are written by an AI model from public web sources and
cited back to their origin. This is information about restaurants, not about you, and we do not
store third-party review text from Google or Yelp.

## How we use information

- To create and secure your account and sign you in
- To show you dishes near you, filtered by your allergens and ranked by your preferences
- To display your reviews and photos to other users
- To moderate content and enforce our Terms of Service
- To operate, maintain, and improve the app
- To respond to you when you contact us
- To comply with legal obligations

To generate AI dish summaries, we send restaurant and dish details and public web content to our
AI provider. **None of your personal information — account, preferences, location, or reviews —
is sent to the AI provider.**

## What we don't do

- We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising
- We do not show ads
- We do not post to, or write back to, Google, Yelp, Apple, or any other platform on your behalf

## Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that run the app for us, and only as needed for
that purpose:

| Provider | What it does | What it receives |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Supabase** | Authentication, database, file storage, server functions | Everything stored in your account |
| **Google** (Maps Platform) | Restaurant lookup near you | Your location at search time; no account data |
| **Google** (Sign-In) | Sign in with Google, if you use it | Handled under Google's privacy policy |
| **Apple** | Sign in with Apple, if you use it | Handled under Apple's privacy policy |
| **Yelp** | Restaurant identity details | Restaurant names and addresses only |
| **Anthropic** | AI-generated dish summaries | Restaurant/dish details and public web content only |

Google Maps features are also subject to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the rights, safety, or property
of our users or others, or as part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case
this policy will continue to apply to your information.

## Data retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account exists.

You may delete your account at any time from **Profile → Delete account** in the app. This
permanently deletes your account, profile, food preferences, reviews, review photos, favorites,
and any reports or blocks you made. If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke DishAdvisor's
Sign in with Apple authorization. It can't be undone. Copies in our providers' routine backups
expire within 30 days. You can
also email abhasetia.studio@gmail.com to request deletion.

## Your choices and rights

- **Access and correction:** you can view and change your food preferences in the app at any
  time. To correct your display name or other account details, email abhasetia.studio@gmail.com.
- **Deletion:** see above.
- **Location:** you can grant or revoke location permission in your device settings at any time.
- Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as to request a copy of your
  data or to object to certain processing. Email abhasetia.studio@gmail.com and we will respond
  within the time the law requires.

## Reporting content

You can report any review you find objectionable from the dish page. Reports record the review,
the reason you chose, and your account, and are visible only to DishAdvisor staff for moderation.
The reported review is hidden for you immediately.

You can also block another user from the same place. You won't see their reviews anymore; they
aren't told. Your list of blocked users is visible only to you, and you can unblock anyone from
**Profile → Blocked users**.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and access to your private data (preferences, favorites, reports,
blocks) is restricted at the database level to your own account. No method of transmission or
storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

## Children's privacy

DishAdvisor is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal
information. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we
will delete it.

## Where your data is processed

DishAdvisor is operated from the United States, and our providers may process your data in the
United States and other countries.

## Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our data practices change and change the effective date at the top.
For material changes, we'll notify you in the app or by email before they take effect.
