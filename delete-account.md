# Delete your DishAdvisor account

DishAdvisor is operated by BAJAJTECHSOLUTIONZ LLC.

## In the app

1. Open DishAdvisor and go to **Profile**.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away. This can't be undone.

## Without the app

Email [abhasetia.studio@gmail.com](mailto:abhasetia.studio@gmail.com) from the email address
on your account, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll confirm by email once your account
is deleted.

## What gets deleted

- Your account and profile (name, email, profile picture)
- Your food preferences (spice tolerance, allergens, favorite cuisines)
- Your reviews, ratings, and review photos
- Your favorites, reports, and blocks
- If you signed in with Apple, DishAdvisor's Sign in with Apple authorization is revoked

Copies in our providers' routine backups expire within 30 days. We don't keep any other data
about you after deletion.

## Deleting some data but keeping your account

You can remove favorites and unblock users in the app at any time. To delete specific reviews or
photos, or any other data, without deleting your account, email
[abhasetia.studio@gmail.com](mailto:abhasetia.studio@gmail.com).
